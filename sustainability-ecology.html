Sustainability & Ecology

SUSTAINABILITY & ECOLOGY

The worldwide concern for sustainable products and services is accelerating. Companies must now integrate sustainability into their products, services and operations to satisfy the changing preferences of consumers and governments.

Just Green production facilities utilize annually renewable natural fibers with progressive manufacturing methods to recycle water waste, reducing pollution and helping to protect the environment. Through the process of composting, products return to fertile soil completing a truly sustainable and ecological solution for today and our future.

For creating and maintaining quality, sanitation and health standards, Just Green facilities operate through fully automated intelligent pulp-molding (no operator-touch) production line systems. The facilities have also adopted low-temperature, normal atmosphere and chlorine-free pulping methods.

Delivering on our commitment to an optimal sustainable environment, milling and production stays near the abundant annually renewable source, supporting the full lifecycle of our products.

Packaging Solutions, made Tree-Free from annually renewable plant fibers that are biodegradable and compostable.

Delivering value through flexibility, function and consistency meeting Your Brand's design, production, packaging, distribution needs.
