MINSTAR HOUSEWARES

Minstar delivers quality household products for everyday living at sensible, reliable, everyday affordable prices

Minstar is our value brand in food storage, kitchen and trash bags. Offerings include a variety of packaging and disposable items made of Aluminum, Paper, and Plastic for preparation, transportation, storage, and serving.
