JUST GREEN PACKAGING

Just Green delivers well-designed, high quality, annually renewable plant fiber packaging solutions at competitive prices for the retail consumer and food packaging industries.

Just Green is synonymous with today's consumer demand for alternative packaging solutions that support sustainability for their planet. By using annually renewable natural plant fibers, we reduce the impact of petroleum-based products in landfills and plastic polluting the oceans.

Just Green packaging and products are made tree-free from varying blends of all-natural pulp fibers; such as Bulrush (reed plant), Wheat Straw, and Bagasse (sugar cane). Depending on your specific packaging strength requirements Bamboo is another renewable raw material option.

As part of our commitment to sustainable alternatives to plastic, paper and Styrofoam, Just Green is working on plastic-alternative solutions. Petroleum-free, using 100% Biodegradable Starch Resin, deriving from sources such as corn and potato starches. This starch resin is formulated to literally replace plastic products through existing plastic resin machinery in any factory worldwide.

Just Green offers a full line of well-designed, high quality products and packaging that naturally do not contain genetically modified organisms (GMO free) and come from annually renewable resources.
